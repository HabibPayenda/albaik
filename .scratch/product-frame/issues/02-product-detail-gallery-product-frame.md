# 02 — The product detail gallery uses the Product frame

**What to build:** On a product page, the main gallery stage is a 4:5 Product frame and its thumbnails are 4:5, so a 1080×1350 photo fills them with no letterbox bars and the whole product is shown.

**Blocked by:** None — can start immediately.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The main gallery stage is a 4:5 Product frame that still fits a laptop viewport, showing the whole photo.
- [ ] The thumbnails use the 4:5 Product frame and show the whole photo.
- [ ] Gallery arrows, the badge, the size chip and image switching still work.
- [ ] When a product has no photo, the Product plate fills the same 4:5 frame.
