# 04 — Compare-at price shows on the card and the detail page

**What to build:** When a product's compare-at price is higher than its price, both the catalogue card and the product detail page show it struck through beside the current price. When it is absent, equal, or lower, nothing extra is shown.

**Blocked by:** None — can start immediately.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A product with a higher compare-at price shows it struck through beside the price on the card and on the detail page.
- [ ] No struck-through figure appears when the compare-at price is absent, equal to, or lower than the price.
- [ ] The card and detail layouts do not break with or without it.
